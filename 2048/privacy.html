Privacy Policy for 2048 Royale

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2048 Royale ("the app"), developed by Alejandro Ramos ("we"), handles information when you use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information such as your name, email address, or location. The app does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your best scores, selected language, color theme, and tile style are saved only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, and general usage data. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. This information is handled by Google according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland), the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy" option in the app's main menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
